## Ranking Competing Explanations

**1. A — Duration / long-end yields (discount rate).** This is the explanation the established desks put first most often, and it is the only mechanism that, in one step, maps a long-end bond shock onto SOX collapsing while equal-weight barely moved. Reuters opened the 18 August close wrap with semiconductors leading “as Middle East uncertainty pushed bond yields to multiyear peaks,” then stated the valuation channel in the third paragraph: rising borrowing costs “lowered how much investors were willing to pay for potential growth in technology profits.” [Reuters via SRN News](https://srnnews.com/us-stock-futures-drop-as-fading-iran-peace-hopes-lift-oil-bond-yields/) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Burns McKinney of NFJ, quoted in that wrap, said “every time bond yields rise, that tends to disproportionately hit the technology names”; Tony Welch of SignatureFD said “there’s nothing that can crack a momentum rally quite like interest rates moving higher.” [Reuters via The Business Times](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/capital-markets-currencies/us-stocks-tech-selloff-weighs-down-wall-street-bond-yields-climb) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. CNBC’s 18 August close lede likewise led with “a run in sovereign bond yields to multi-year highs amid concerns of persistent inflation and elevated oil prices,” with the semiconductor pullback as the equity expression, not as an independent earnings event. [CNBC 18 Aug live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. CNN’s same-day bond explainer recorded the 30-year at 5.34%, its highest since 2007, and the 10-year at 4.74%, and stated that a surge in yields “can also put pressure on the stock market” by altering “analysts’ calculations for the value of stocks,” citing the Nasdaq’s 1.3% drop. [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/investing/global-bond-market) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart made the same channel explicit in its 18 August pre-market note: higher yields “raise the discount rate applied to future earnings.” [Barchart pre-market](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3902047/nasdaq-futures-plunge-as-rising-bond-yields-drive-chip-selloff) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Two tape details tighten this from a generic “rates are up” story to a long-end duration story. AP recorded the 10-year edging *down* on Tuesday to 4.70% from 4.72% even as the 30-year stayed near its 2007 high, which means the equity hit coincided with the long end, not with a parallel backup in the policy-sensitive 10-year. [AP 18 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-war-iran-trump-a805333e767251279e68e0686b28a8c9) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart confirmed the 10-year tagged a 1.5-year high of 4.75% intraday then fell 2 bp to 4.70% into the close, and that stocks “recovered from their worst level… after bond yields fell from their highs.” [Barchart 18 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3915336/stocks-finish-lower-as-chipmakers-and-ai-stocks-fall) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Wednesday is the in-sample test: after Treasury said it would at least double long-end liquidity-support buybacks, the 30-year fell more than the 10-year (AP: 5.28% to 5.18%; 10-year 4.71% to 4.64%), the S&P 500 and equal-weight bounced, and gold miners ripped — all the duration-sensitive trades other than chips behaved as the discount-rate channel predicts. [AP 19 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-futures-bonds-oil-inflation-trump-ai-9e355be634e4a21059fc1d1f20a1239e) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Reuters’ Robert Pavlik quote on Wednesday restated the same DCF arithmetic: “Most tech companies are priced mainly on forward earnings expectations and when interest rates creep up, those expectations are worth less.” [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/wall-st-up-after-tech-selloff-as-yields-retreat-moderna-surges-ce7859d2da8af621) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. What the duration channel does *not* fully explain is Wednesday’s residual: SOXX still fell more than 2% after yields dropped. That leftover is why A ranks first as the *initiating* cause of the two-day SOX drawdown but not as a complete description of 19 August. **Falsifier:** SOX recovering to 17 August levels while the 30-year holds near 5.3%, or equal-weight duration names (utilities, staples, software) falling as much as SOX on a day when only the long end moves. Wednesday’s chip continuation after the 30-year broke lower is already a partial falsifier of “duration is sufficient.”

**2. G — Profit-taking after the extreme YTD chip/memory run.** This is the co-lede that explains why the duration shock landed on SOX rather than on Mag7 or software. Reuters did not use the words “profit-taking,” but it described the behavior: investors “fled stocks that had rallied previously on booming AI-related demand,” Micron fell 7% “after rising almost 18% in the previous five sessions,” and the Roundhill Memory ETF tumbled 8.8% “after five straight gains.” [Reuters via The Business Times](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/capital-markets-currencies/us-stocks-tech-selloff-weighs-down-wall-street-bond-yields-climb) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. AP put the crowding claim in the lede itself: AI winners had been “veering up and down this summer on worries that their prices shot too high,” and “even with their recent swings… Micron has more than tripled this year.” [AP 18 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-war-iran-trump-a805333e767251279e68e0686b28a8c9) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart’s 18 August close listed Sandisk and Seagate down more than 9% and the memory/storage complex as the tape, then recorded software — Intuit, Adobe, Salesforce — *rising* the same day, which is the opposite of a broad tech-duration wipeout and is what a crowded-hardware unwind looks like. [Barchart 18 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3915336/stocks-finish-lower-as-chipmakers-and-ai-stocks-fall) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. That split is why G ranks immediately under A as an explanation of SOX versus equal-weight: RSP is not loaded with five-day memory winners; SOX is. G also helps with Wednesday. If the Tuesday smash was only a discount-rate reset, chips should have bounced with the 30-year; Barchart instead printed a “second day” of chip and AI-infrastructure selling, with Seagate leading S&P 500 losers down more than 7%. [Barchart 19 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3937543/stocks-finish-mixed-as-bond-yields-fall-and-chipmakers-slide) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. **Falsifier:** equal-weighted, non-extended chip names (or SOX ex-memory) falling as hard as Sandisk/Seagate/Micron; or a session in which the hottest five-day winners outperform the rest of SOX.

**3. B — Iran-Hormuz-oil inflation risk.** In the Reuters lede this is not a competing cause; it is the *spark* for A. McKinney’s quoted chain is talks break down → oil up → inflation expectations up → bond yields rise → tech is hit. [Reuters via SRN News](https://srnnews.com/us-stock-futures-drop-as-fading-iran-peace-hopes-lift-oil-bond-yields/) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. That chain explains energy’s +1.8% on Tuesday, which Reuters attributed “with support from rising oil prices” after Iran threatened a “fully offensive” posture and Washington ruled out extending a ceasefire. [Reuters via The Business Times](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/capital-markets-currencies/us-stocks-tech-selloff-weighs-down-wall-street-bond-yields-climb) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. AP located the *level* of yields in the war: the 10-year “remains well above its 3.97% level from just before the war with Iran began,” and “yields have jumped since the war began because high oil prices are pushing upward on inflation,” with Brent at $91.02 versus $72.87 before the war. [AP 18 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-war-iran-trump-a805333e767251279e68e0686b28a8c9) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart devoted several paragraphs to the closed Strait of Hormuz, Trump declining to extend the expired Iran agreement, the UAE cutting ties after alleged Iranian missiles, and WTI at a three-week high. [Barchart 18 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3915336/stocks-finish-lower-as-chipmakers-and-ai-stocks-fall) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**; [Barchart 19 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3937543/stocks-finish-mixed-as-bond-yields-fall-and-chipmakers-slide) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. CNN treated the war and Brent at $91 as an exacerbating factor that leads investors to demand higher yields “to compensate for the risk of inflation eating into their return.” [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/investing/global-bond-market) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Ranked as a standalone cause of the SOX/RSP split, B is weaker than A. A broad oil-inflation shock that raised the cost of capital for the whole economy should have leaned on equal-weight cyclicals; RSP was nearly unchanged across 13–19 August and *rose* on Wednesday while oil was still firm. The pattern that actually printed — energy up with oil, gold miners down Tuesday then up Wednesday as the long end reversed, SOX uniquely weak — is oil as the inflation input to the long-end, not oil as a risk-off that flattened the whole tape. **Falsifier:** Hormuz reopens and oil drops while SOX keeps falling *and* energy fails to give back; or a session in which oil spikes and RSP falls in line with SOX.

**4. C — AI/hyperscaler debt crowding and off-balance-sheet commitments (~$3T WSJ).** The crowding *channel* is in the 18 August bond explainers; the $3 trillion figure is not. CNN wrote that government bonds are “under pressure from a wave of new corporate debt, including tech firms focused on artificial intelligence,” and quoted deVere’s Nigel Green: “Hyperscaler borrowing to fund AI infrastructure is competing for the same pool of buyers at the same moment governments need those buyers most.” [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/investing/global-bond-market) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. CNBC’s Jeff Cox, embedded in the 18 August live blog, listed “a rash of corporate debt issuance competing with Treasurys for investors’ favor” among the June-to-August drivers of the long end, alongside the deficit and above-target inflation. [CNBC 18 Aug live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. AP’s 18 August wrap made the related, narrower claim that “high yields could also slow the borrowing Big Tech companies are doing to pay for data centers.” [AP 18 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-war-iran-trump-a805333e767251279e68e0686b28a8c9) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. The Wall Street Journal analysis dated 16 August 2026 — nine firms, about $3 trillion of off-balance-sheet AI commitments, roughly $1.9 trillion purchase contracts and $1.2 trillion uncommenced leases versus about $600 billion trailing capex — was not named, quoted, or numbered in the Reuters, AP, CNBC, Barchart, or CNN *session wraps* retrieved for 18–20 August. [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/why-big-techs-ai-spending-is-3-trillion-higher-than-it-seems-e1067bb2) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: Medium) **Established**, paywalled; figure corroborated by [FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2026/08/19/trillions-of-dollars-in-ai-spending-not-in-the-balance-sheet-yet/) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: Medium) **Low/secondary**. As an explanation of the SOX/RSP/energy pattern, crowding is a plausible *reason the long end is the pressure point* and a reason the equity expression is AI-infrastructure rather than all growth. It is a poorer match for Tuesday’s Mag7 split: Apple and Microsoft rose, which is awkward if the borrowers themselves were the story, and it does not explain energy’s Tuesday outperformance except through the yield channel already counted in A. **Falsifier:** corporate IG issuance and hyperscaler concessions ease, the 30-year still rallies, and SOX still underperforms; or Mag7 borrowers (Meta, Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft) falling harder than the chip suppliers that are on the other side of the purchase commitments.

**5. F — OpenAI losses / AI ROI scare.** Two different claims are often collapsed here, and the wraps keep them apart. The *thematic* ROI scare is AP’s 18 August lede: AI winners were sinking on worries “that the strong demand for memory, processors and other building blocks of data centers may fizzle out if AI proves less profitable than promised.” [AP 18 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-war-iran-trump-a805333e767251279e68e0686b28a8c9) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Reuters’ 19 August copy carried the same background doubt: “doubts lingered about whether hefty AI spending can yield tangible results soon.” [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/wall-st-up-after-tech-selloff-as-yields-retreat-moderna-surges-ce7859d2da8af621) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. The *specific OpenAI print* is a Wednesday story. CNBC’s 19 August lede said “other tech stocks slid after The Wall Street Journal reported, citing sources, that OpenAI disclosed second-quarter results that disappointed investors,” with revenue up 18% quarter-on-quarter but losses also growing; Broadcom fell more than 4% and AMD nearly 4%. [CNBC 19 Aug live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Anadolu, summarizing the same session, wrote that Broadcom and AMD “fell around 3.7% following reports that OpenAI’s second-quarter losses increased despite revenue growth.” [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/economy/us-stocks-end-higher-as-treasury-buybacks-ease-pressure-on-bond-yields/4032240) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: Medium) **Established**. That timing means OpenAI can explain part of Wednesday’s chip residual after yields fell; it cannot be the cause of Tuesday’s ~5% SOX crash, when all 30 SOX names were already down and CNBC/Reuters/Barchart named yields and memory-extension, not OpenAI. Thematic ROI also fails to explain energy up and gold miners’ Wednesday melt-up. **Falsifier:** attributing Tuesday’s SOX drop to the OpenAI print; or Nvidia, the name most levered to OpenAI compute, falling more than memory/HDD on Tuesday (Reuters had Nvidia −2.3% versus Micron −7% and Sandisk −9%).

**6. H — Treasury buyback as a failed rescue for tech.** This is not a cause of the decline. It is the 19 August policy response, and the wraps treat it as a test that the long-end bid helped the *index* and *miners* but did not reverse chips. CNBC led Wednesday with the S&P 500 ending a three-day losing run “as bond yields fell following the Treasury Department’s announcement,” then noted the rally faded from +0.7% session highs and that chips/OpenAI still slid. [CNBC 19 Aug live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. AP led with the same buyback, then quoted BNP Paribas that the size “is a fraction of the overall total” and that buybacks are “necessary, but not sufficient” against “challenged Fed credibility.” [AP 19 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-futures-bonds-oil-inflation-trump-ai-9e355be634e4a21059fc1d1f20a1239e) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. CNBC’s Ben Emons note, carried in the live blog, said investors were misreading the operation as QE or yield-curve control when it is “liquidity housekeeping, not an outright purchase program.” [CNBC 19 Aug live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart is the cleanest statement of the failed-rescue-for-tech claim without using those words: the broader market was “supported… by lower US bond yields” after the $2 billion-to-at-least-$4 billion long-end buyback, “however, weakness in chipmaker and AI-infrastructure stocks continued for a second day,” while mining stocks “rallied sharply” on the same announcement. [Barchart 19 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3937543/stocks-finish-mixed-as-bond-yields-fall-and-chipmakers-slide) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Ranked as an *explanation of why tech fell*, H is circular. Ranked as an explanation of the *cross-asset pattern on Wednesday*, it is useful: gold miners and RSP/S&P responded to the duration relief; SOX did not, which is why G and F sit above H as accounts of the residual. **Falsifier:** SOX bouncing in line with the 30-year and with GDX on 19 August; that would have made the buyback a successful tech rescue and would have strengthened pure-A.

**7. D — Fed hawkish minutes / hike risk.** The minutes of the 28–29 July FOMC were released at 2 p.m. Eastern on Wednesday 19 August, after Tuesday’s SOX crash and after Wednesday morning’s buyback-driven yield drop. Reuters on Tuesday mentioned them only as a *forthcoming* event. [Reuters via The Business Times](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/capital-markets-currencies/us-stocks-tech-selloff-weighs-down-wall-street-bond-yields-climb) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. The document itself was hawkish in the conditional sense AP and CNBC reported: “many participants assessed that policy tightening would likely be necessary if inflation did not decline,” after a 9–3 hold at about 3.5%–3.75%. [AP minutes](https://apnews.com/article/federal-reserve-rates-inflation-warsh-0030d04832b89b564b743b34ab2cd8de) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**; [CNBC minutes](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/fed-minutes-july-2026-officials-saw-need-for-rate-hike-if-inflation-doesnt-cool.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart called the minutes “slightly hawkish” and printed the market as discounting a 32% chance of a 25 bp hike on 15–16 September, down from 35% on Tuesday — the opposite of a minutes-driven hike scare. [Barchart 19 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3937543/stocks-finish-mixed-as-bond-yields-fall-and-chipmakers-slide) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**; [Barchart 18 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3915336/stocks-finish-lower-as-chipmakers-and-ai-stocks-fall) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. CNBC’s minutes story added that, after recent inflation data, “market pricing switched to an expectation for the Fed to stay on hold likely until December,” having previously expected September. [CNBC minutes](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/fed-minutes-july-2026-officials-saw-need-for-rate-hike-if-inflation-doesnt-cool.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Welch’s Tuesday comment that rising yields suggested Fed policy was “too easy” is a long-end/term-premium view, not a minutes event. A short-rate hike scare would also have shown up in the 2-year and in equal-weight, not uniquely in SOX. AP on Wednesday was explicit that 10- and 30-year yields “are less beholden to the Federal Reserve.” [AP 19 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-futures-bonds-oil-inflation-trump-ai-9e355be634e4a21059fc1d1f20a1239e) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. **Falsifier already observed:** the largest down day occurred before the minutes; hike odds did not jump after them; the 2-year was not the moving part of the curve.

**8. E — Company earnings misses (Meta, Tesla, Apple guide).** Late-July results are real background, and they are absent from the 18–20 August wrap ledes as a cause. Barchart’s 18 and 19 August closes treated Q2 earnings as a *bullish* offset: 85% of S&P 500 reporters beating, tracking ~32% earnings growth, with AI infrastructure set to contribute nearly 60% of S&P 500 EPS growth. [Barchart 18 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3915336/stocks-finish-lower-as-chipmakers-and-ai-stocks-fall) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. AP attributed Meta’s 4.4% drop on Tuesday to opening statements in a California children’s-safety trial, not to the July EPS miss. [AP 18 Aug](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-war-iran-trump-a805333e767251279e68e0686b28a8c9) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. Barchart repeated that Meta attribution. [Barchart 18 Aug](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3915336/stocks-finish-lower-as-chipmakers-and-ai-stocks-fall) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**. The 18–19 August tape also ran against E: Apple was green both days in the CNBC/TradingKey close lists that match Yahoo (+1.45% Tuesday, +2.19% Wednesday), and Tesla slipped 0.72% Tuesday then rose 4.23% Wednesday — not the signature of an earnings-miss wreck still driving SOX. [CNBC 18 Aug](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) (accessed 2026-08-20, confidence: High) **Established**; Barchart 19 Aug software/mega-cap bounce. **Falsifier already observed:** the names with the cited misses were not the leaders of the 18–19 August SOX smash; memory and HDD were.

**Lede-order scoreboard (18 August close wraps only).** Reuters: B then A, with G in the third paragraph and energy as the opposite-side check.
