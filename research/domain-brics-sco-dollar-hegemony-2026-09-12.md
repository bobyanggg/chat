# Research Report — 金磚與上合是否在挑戰美元與美國霸權

> **Type:** domain | **Date:** 2026-09-12 | **Constraints:** 全球；證據窗 2001–2026；展望 2030–2035
>
> **Assumptions:** type=domain（國際政治經濟學／地緣制度），並在 Step 4 加上趨勢情境層。範圍=全球。時間=以 2009 年金磚峰會與 2001 年上合成立為起點，重點核對 2022 年俄烏戰爭後的加速訊號，展望至 2030–2035。「金磚四國」按使用者用語保留，但分析對象是已擴員的 BRICS（2026 年官方列 11 個正式成員），不是停在 2009 年的四國。「挑戰美元／美國霸權」拆成三個可檢驗目標：(a) 取代美元作為主要儲備、計價、支付貨幣；(b) 削弱美國把金融體系武器化（制裁、SWIFT 切斷）的能力；(c) 在安全與規則制定上削弱美國主導的秩序。成功率=未來約十年內出現「物質上、可持久」的取代，而不是只停留在多極化修辭。

> - 分清官方修辭與實際制度建設
> - 用儲備、支付、貿易計價、石油計價、制裁繞行等硬指標衡量進展

## Domain Overview

### Industry Structure

BRICS and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) are often discussed as one anti-Western bloc. They are institutionally different. BRICS is an informal rotating-presidency consultation group with no founding treaty, permanent secretariat, or headquarters. The SCO is a treaty organisation with a 2002 Charter, a budget, a Beijing secretariat, and a Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS) in Tashkent. [SCO, “About”](https://eng.sectsco.org/20170109/192193.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [ISDP BRICS backgrounder](https://www.isdp.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Backgrounder-BRICS-Oct-19.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

India’s 2026 BRICS presidency and a 10 September 2026 Press Information Bureau note list eleven full members: Brazil, China, Egypt, Ethiopia, India, Indonesia, Iran, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and the United Arab Emirates. The same official Indian sources say these members account for 49.5 percent of world population, 40 percent of global GDP, and 26 percent of global trade, and that ten Partner Countries joined in 2025: Belarus, Bolivia, Cuba, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Nigeria, Thailand, Uganda, Uzbekistan, and Vietnam. [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?lang=1&ModuleId=3&NoteId=159946&reg=3) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [BRICS 2026 Presidency](https://www.brics2026.gov.in/about-us/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

**Saudi Arabia is the main membership conflict.** Official chairs list the Kingdom as a full member. Reuters reported on 8 May 2025 that Riyadh had “held off formally joining,” and Carnegie documented the same hedge. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/saudi-arabia-sits-fence-over-brics-with-eye-vital-ties-with-us-2025-05-08/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Carnegie](https://carnegieendowment.org/emissary/2024/11/brics-saudi-arabia-hedging-why) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Any GDP or oil-share figure that treats Saudi Arabia as locked-in overstates cohesion.

The SCO official site lists ten members: Belarus, India, Iran, Kazakhstan, China, Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan, Russia, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan; two observers (Afghanistan, Mongolia); and about fifteen dialogue partners. [SCO About](https://eng.sectsco.org/20170109/192193.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. Tianjin 2025 adopted Decision No. 4 to merge observer and dialogue-partner status into a single “SCO Partner” category; Bishkek 2026 still used the old labels in ceremonial practice. [SCO observers page](https://eng.sectsco.org/20260306/2185663.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [SCO Bishkek communiqué](https://eng.sectsco.org/20260901/2493609.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

BRIC’s first leaders’ summit was Yekaterinburg, 16 June 2009. The Joint Statement asked for IMF and World Bank reform and “a more democratic and just multi-polar world order,” while still placing the UN and G20 at the centre. [Chinese MFA archive](https://brics2022.mfa.gov.cn/eng/hywj/ODS/202203/t20220308_10649520.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. South Africa joined in 2010. Johannesburg 2023 invited Argentina, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE from 1 January 2024; Argentina declined. [Johannesburg II Declaration](https://www.ndb.int/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/XV-BRICS-Summit-Johannesburg-II-Declaration-24-August-2023.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2023/dec/29/milei-argentina-brics-bloc) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Indonesia became a full member on 6 January 2025. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/indonesia-join-brics-bloc-full-member-brazil-says-2025-01-06/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

The SCO traces to the Shanghai Five (1996–97), the 15 June 2001 Shanghai Declaration, and the 7 June 2002 Charter. Official goals are regional peace, the “three evils” (terrorism, separatism, extremism), and economic cooperation. The Organisation “does not have the attributes of a defence alliance.” [SCO About](https://eng.sectsco.org/20170109/192193.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. India and Pakistan joined in 2017, Iran in 2023, Belarus in 2024. [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/belarus-joins-shanghai-cooperation-organization-as-10th-member-state/3265708) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

BRICS’ only treaty financial institutions are the New Development Bank and the Contingent Reserve Arrangement, both signed at Fortaleza on 15 July 2014. The NDB mobilises resources for infrastructure “to complement the existing efforts of multilateral and regional financial institutions.” Initial authorised capital is USD 100 billion. [NDB Agreement](https://www.ndb.int/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Agreement-on-the-New-Development-Bank.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. Effective NDB members as of June 2026 are not the same as BRICS political members: Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Bangladesh, UAE, Egypt, Algeria, and Uzbekistan. Iran, Indonesia, and Saudi Arabia are not effective NDB members. [NDB Uzbekistan accession](https://www.ndb.int/news/uzbekistan-officially-joins-new-development-bank-as-new-member-country/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [NDB Investor Presentation, June 2026](https://www.ndb.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/New-Development-Bank_Investor-Presentation_June_2026-2.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

The CRA is a USD 100 billion self-managed swap network, not a paid-in fund and not a currency. China commits USD 41 billion; Brazil, Russia, and India USD 18 billion each; South Africa USD 5 billion. Only 30 percent of access is de-linked from the IMF; 70 percent requires an on-track IMF programme. Brazil’s 2025 site said no participant had drawn CRA resources. [CRA Treaty](https://nanjing.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815064113831/index.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [Brazil BRICS NDB/CRA page](https://brics.br/en/about-the-brics/new-development-bank) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: Medium) **Primary**.

Kazan 2024 welcomed local-currency use and a “voluntary and non-binding” BRICS Cross-Border Payments Initiative (BCBPI), and agreed only to “discuss and study the feasibility” of BRICS Clear. Rio 2025 did not launch a live common currency or SWIFT replacement. [Kazan Declaration](https://cdn.brics-russia2024.ru/upload/docs/Kazan_Declaration_FINAL.pdf?1729693488349783) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [Rio Declaration 2025](https://bricsthinktankscouncil.org/knowledge-base/17th-brics-summit-rio-de-janeiro-declaration-2025/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

Tianjin 2025 said “interested member states decided to establish” an SCO Development Bank. Bishkek 2026 produced no founding agreement, capital key, or headquarters decision. [Tianjin Declaration](http://www.en.kremlin.ru/supplement/6376) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [Times of Central Asia](https://timesca.com/sco-summit-delivers-institutional-reform-but-leaves-development-bank-unsettled/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

Full dual members are China, Russia, India, and Iran. Pakistan is SCO-only. Brazil, South Africa, Ethiopia, and Indonesia are BRICS-only. Decision rules in both clubs are consensus. No sources found for a formal joint BRICS–SCO programme, treaty, or payment platform whose stated purpose is to replace the dollar.

### Core Terminology

An international money occupies several distinct cells: reserve currency, invoicing currency, vehicle/payment currency, and store of value, at both private and official levels. [Cohen, *Currency Power*](http://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/s10577.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. A currency can lose ground in one cell while remaining dominant in another. The Federal Reserve’s 2023 survey found the dollar still “outsized” relative to US GDP on every measure it tracks: about 58 percent of disclosed official reserves, the dominant invoicing currency outside Europe, about 60 percent of international banking claims, and the principal crisis-funding currency via Fed swap lines. [Bertaut, von Beschwitz and Curcuru, Fed FEDS Notes](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/the-international-role-of-the-us-dollar-post-covid-edition-20230623.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

No sources found for a formal IMF definition of “dollar hegemony.” Academic IPE uses the phrase for the dollar’s top-currency position plus the political capacity it confers. [Eichengreen, *Exorbitant Privilege*](https://books.google.com/books/about/Exorbitant_Privilege.html?id=FqTBbvyb9NwC) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. “Exorbitant privilege” itself has at least three meanings: Giscard’s 1965 cheap-deficit-finance claim; Gourinchas–Rey’s excess return on the US net international investment position; and popular seigniorage talk. They do not rise or fall together. [Gourinchas and Rey](http://www.piketty.pse.ens.fr/files/GourinchasRey2007.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [IMF WP/24/241](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/wp/2024/english/wpiea2024241-print-pdf.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

A reserve currency is an official-sector store of value that is “freely usable.” [IMF BPM6](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/bop/2007/pdf/chap6.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. After a 2025 methodological change that imputes previously unallocated reserves, the ECB reports the dollar still about 57 percent of reserves in 2025, the euro about 20 percent, and the renminbi “close to 2 percent.” [ECB IRE, June 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/ire/ecb.ire202606.en.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

SWIFT is a messaging cooperative, not a settlement system. [Deutsche Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/tasks/payment-systems/oversight/swift-626536) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. Correspondent banking is the ledger layer: cutting a bank off from US correspondent accounts, not just SWIFT messages, is what denies dollar settlement. [BIS CPMI](https://www.bis.org/cpmi/paysysinfo/corr_bank_data.htm) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. CIPS is China’s RMB clearinghouse, analogous to CHIPS, not a SWIFT replacement. [CIPS](https://www.cips.com.cn/en/about_us/about_cips/introduction/index.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [Eichengreen for CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/sanctions-swift-and-chinas-cross-border-interbank-payments-system) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. SPFS is Russia’s messaging system and still needs correspondent relations. [Bank of Russia](https://cbr.ru/eng/development/mcirabis/fin_msg_transfer_system/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. OFAC treats joining SPFS as a sanctions-risk. [OFAC SPFS alert](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/933656/download?inline=) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

De-dollarization, diversification, and weaponization of finance are not interchangeable. IMF work finds the dollar’s reserve-share decline since 2000 came mainly from “active portfolio diversification,” with about one quarter of the lost share going to the RMB and three quarters to nontraditional currencies. [Arslanalp, Eichengreen and Simpson-Bell, IMF WP/22/58](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/wp/2022/english/wpiea2022058-print-pdf.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. Weaponized interdependence is Farrell and Newman’s theory that states with jurisdiction over network hubs can surveil (panopticon) and exclude (chokepoint). Their cases include SWIFT. [Farrell and Newman, *International Security* 2019](https://direct.mit.edu/isec/article/44/1/42/12237/Weaponized-Interdependence-How-Global-Economic) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

US hegemony is larger than dollar dominance. Susan Strange identified four structures—security, production, finance, and knowledge. [Strange, “The Persistent Myth of Lost Hegemony”](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0020818300027600) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Carla Norrlof treats US advantages in trade, money, and security as mutually reinforcing, including a “security premium”: allies protected by the United States have incentives to hold dollars. [Norrlof, House testimony, 2022](https://www.congress.gov/117/meeting/house/115144/witnesses/HHRG-117-BA10-Wstate-NorrlfC-20220920.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Secondary sanctions are the legal hinge: OFAC can cut non-US banks off from US correspondent accounts for dealing with blocked persons. [OFAC](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/935656/download?inline=) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

This suggests BRICS and the SCO can touch bilateral invoicing and settlement among willing pairs, and can thicken correspondent networks that do not clear in New York. They cannot, on present evidence, touch the dollar’s FX vehicle role, the depth of the Treasury market, Fed swap lines, US jurisdiction over correspondent accounts, or the security and technology structures of hegemony.

> Conflicts noted: “Exorbitant privilege” has incompatible definitions. Triffin’s gold-convertibility dilemma is not the later current-account version. CIPS is routinely mislabeled a “SWIFT alternative.”
> Gaps: No official IMF definition of dollar hegemony, petrodollar system, or international de-dollarization. No operating specification of BRICS Clear as of 12 September 2026.

### Current State and Trajectory

On the latest IMF COFER reading, the dollar’s share of official FX reserves was 57.13 percent in 2026Q1; the euro was 20.03 percent; the renminbi was 1.99 percent. [IMF Data Brief, July 2026](https://data.imf.org/en/news/imf%20data%20brief%20july%201) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [ECB IRE, June 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/ire/ecb.ire202606.en.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. The same series put the dollar near 71 percent around 1999–2001 and about 65 percent at end-2014. [IMF Annual Report 2005](https://www.imf.org/-/media/websites/imf/imported-flagship-issues/external/pubs/ft/ar/2005/eng/pdf/_file7pdf.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [Arslanalp et al., IMF WP/22/58](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/wp/2022/english/wpiea2022058-print-pdf.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. From 2025Q3 the IMF imputes previously unallocated reserves, so older “allocated” percentages are not strictly the same object. The reserve picture is slow diversification, not a post-2022 collapse. Most of the 14-point loss since 2001 happened before Russia’s full-scale invasion. The RMB has not absorbed the lost share.

In FX markets the dollar has strengthened. The BIS 2025 Triennial Survey found the dollar on one side of 89.2 percent of OTC FX trades in April 2025, up from 88.4 percent in 2022. The RMB rose to 8.5 percent from 7.0 percent. [BIS](https://www.bis.org/statistics/rpfx25_fx.htm) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

SWIFT payment shares sit in a different band. The January 2026 tracker put the RMB at 3.13 percent of global payment value and 2.42 percent of international payments excluding intra-eurozone traffic. [SWIFT Global Currency Tracker, January 2026](https://www.swift.com/swift-resource/252544/download) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. Trade-finance messages remain about 80–83 percent dollar and about 5.5–8.4 percent RMB. [Trade Treasury Payments, June–July 2026](https://tradetreasurypayments.com/articles/usd-dominates-the-global-currency-and-trade-finance-market-swifts-global-currency-tracker-june-2026) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: Medium) **Established**. China’s own goods-trade RMB settlement was 27.2 percent in 2024, a China-centric figure, not a global invoicing share. [PBOC 2025 RMB report, reprinted](https://www.sohu.com/a/951679514_122020073) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: Medium) **Established**.

An IMF–ECB invoicing dataset through 2023 finds global invoicing shares “broadly stable” and “no robust evidence consistent with effective policy initiatives to reduce dollar reliance in oil exports.” [Boz et al., IMF WP/25/178](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/wp/2025/english/wpiea2025178-source-pdf.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. The ECB puts the dollar at about 40 percent of global export invoicing including the euro area and about 60 percent excluding it. [ECB IRE, June 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/other-publications/ire/article/html/ecb.ireart202506_02~a8e66f5ea3.en.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. No official IEA/OPEC/IMF/BIS series publishes a 2024–2026 global share of oil contracts priced in dollars. Treat circulating “nearly 80 percent” figures as Low.

Mainland China’s reported Treasury holdings were $633.4 billion at end-June 2026, down from $867.1 billion in December 2022 and a TIC-derived peak of $1,316.7 billion in November 2013. India held $186.4 billion and Brazil $168.4 billion. [US Treasury TIC Table 5](https://ticdata.treasury.gov/Publish/slt_table5.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. Russia does not appear on the monthly major-holders list after dumping most holdings in 2018; post-2022, US-located Russian sovereign assets were on the order of $5 billion inside a much larger frozen G7/EU stock. [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-is-the-status-of-russias-frozen-sovereign-assets/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

Local-currency settlement is real and large on the Russia–China corridor: Russian officials said ruble-plus-yuan settlement exceeded 90 percent in 2024 and 99.1 percent by November 2025. [TASS](https://tass.com/economy/1789111) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. For Brazil–China, an academic compilation of official statistics for 2019–2023 found 95.38 percent of Brazilian export receipts still in dollars and the RMB only 0.35 percent of import payments. [SciELO / RBPI](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbpi/a/3HPZn3K5Qt53fVxN5Sz466v/?lang=en) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. No official intra-BRICS bloc-wide settlement share for 2024–2026 was found.

A BRICS common currency was discussed and not created. Kazan did not launch one. In February 2025 Brazilian officials told Reuters the presidency would not advance a common currency. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/currencies/brazil-nixes-brics-currency-eyes-less-reliance-mighty-dollar-2025-02-13/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

Official gold buying was the largest multi-year wave in decades: about 1,082 tonnes in 2022, 1,037 in 2023, about 1,045–1,092 in 2024, and 863 in 2025, then a cooler first half of 2026. [World Gold Council FY tables](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-full-year-2025/central-banks) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. The IMF and ECB both state that gold’s share of total official reserves overtook US Treasuries in 2025, but almost all of that crossover is the gold-price surge, not an equivalent Treasury sale. [IMF Data Brief, July 2026](https://data.imf.org/en/news/imf%20data%20brief%20july%201) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. [ECB IRE, June 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/ire/ecb.ire202606.en.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

Institutional consensus at IMF, BIS, ECB, Brookings, Carnegie, Bruegel, and the Atlantic Council is that de-dollarization is real at the margin, slow in the core, and often overstated. [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/is-the-us-dollars-reserve-currency-status-eroding/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Carnegie, June 2026](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2026/06/the-future-of-american-economic-power) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Atlantic Council Dollar Dominance Monitor](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/programs/geoeconomics-center/dollar-dominance-monitor/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. An NBER paper co-authored by Eichengreen in 2025–26 concludes that BRICS de-dollarization plans and mBridge-style platforms are “not likely to have an immediate impact on the evolution of the international monetary and reserve system in the immediate future.” [NBER WP 34478](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w34478/w34478.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

> Conflicts noted: SWIFT payment shares differ by whether intra-eurozone traffic is included. COFER has a 2025 methodological break. TIC “China, Mainland” is a custody concept. Russia–China 90–99 percent local-currency claims are official bilateral figures, not a BRICS aggregate.

### Established Best Practices

The sterling-to-dollar transition is the only fully observed modern case in which one paper reserve currency displaced another. [Eichengreen, Tawney Lecture](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~eichengr/research/tawney_lecture2apr29-05.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Dating is contested. The conventional story has a 30–70 year lag from US economic primacy to postwar reserve leadership. The “new view” finds a first reserve crossing around 1924, a first trade-credit crossing in 1914–20, a sterling rebound in the early 1930s, and a durable dollar lead only after 1945. [Eichengreen and Flandreau, NBER WP 14154](https://www.nber.org/papers/w14154) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

Necessary conditions in the literature are fairly stable: issuer size, inflation credibility, exchange-rate stability, and especially depth of the home financial centre. [Chinn and Frankel, NBER WP 11510](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w11510/w11510.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Military power and rule of law enter as reinforcing conditions. A military alliance raised a partner’s currency share in pre-1914 reserves by about 30 percentage points. [Eichengreen, Mehl and Chițu, NBER WP 24145](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24145) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

Kindleberger’s standard for hegemony is not reserve share. A hegemon must keep markets open for distress goods, supply counter-cyclical long-term lending, and discount in crisis. [Kindleberger, *The World in Depression*](https://web.archive.org/web/20080310145946/http:/www.mtholyoke.edu/acad/intrel/depress.htm) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. In 2008 the Fed became the global dollar lender of last resort, providing just under $4.5 trillion through swap lines; standing unlimited lines among major central banks were made permanent in 2013. India, Russia, and China were not granted equivalent standing lines. [Tooze, *Foreign Affairs*](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/articles/world/forgotten-history-financial-crisis-adam-tooze) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

The euro is the cleanest modern failure to dethrone the dollar. Its allocated reserve share peaked at 28 percent in 2009, then fell after the sovereign-debt crisis. [Jost and Rösl](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9395879/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. The standard explanation is the missing common safe asset: US marketable Treasuries exceeded $14 trillion in 2018 versus about $3–4 trillion of non-crisis euro-area sovereigns. [Ilzetzki, Reinhart and Rogoff](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w26760/w26760.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. The published record does not contain a case in which a currency bloc without a single fiscal authority became the *leading* reserve currency.

The yen peaked around 7–9 percent of reserves around 1990–92 and never approached leadership. [Chinn and Frankel](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w11510/w11510.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Takagi, BIS Papers](https://www.bis.org/publ/bppdf/bispap61g.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. The SDR is not used to invoice trade or denominate private bonds; at end-2024 IMF-related assets were about 6.1 percent of total international reserves and are excluded from COFER FX reserves. [IMF Annual Report 2025 appendices](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/ar/2025/pdfs/imf-annual-report-2025-appendices.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**.

Security-order analogues are not interchangeable with currency analogues. The Warsaw Pact succeeded as a coercive alliance and failed once coercion was withdrawn. The Non-Aligned Movement succeeded as a diplomatic identity and failed at the New International Economic Order. Belt and Road is a connectivity program, not a Marshall Plan and not a reserve-currency project. A club that holds summits can “succeed” on NAM terms while failing every monetary test.

**Falsifiable success scorecard** (strong, sterling-to-dollar sense; crossings must hold for about a decade):

- Official reserve leadership: challenger exceeds the dollar’s COFER share, or dollar falls below 40 percent *and* challenger exceeds 40 percent.
- Vehicle-currency leadership: challenger on one side of more than half of global FX turnover.
- Invoicing as a vehicle, not just bilateral, currency: challenger’s global invoicing share substantially exceeds the issuer’s trade share.
- Safe-asset supply on the order of the US Treasury market that does not shrink in a crisis.
- Crisis backstop: challenger’s central bank extends standing, actually drawn swap lines to third countries.
- Network/chokepoint test: a material share of ordinary third-country payments completed without dollar clearing or SWIFT.
- Security premium: protector-state’s currency share in allies’ reserves far above pecuniary prediction.
- Convertibility: non-residents can enter and exit deep markets without capital-account permission.
- Hegemony, not just currency: Kindleberger public goods plus Strange’s four structures.

> Conflicts noted: Dating of dollar overtaking sterling; network-effects winner-take-all versus Eichengreen’s bipolar “new view”; euro and yen peak shares differ by dataset.
> Gaps: No second completed paper-reserve transition, so out-of-sample inference is under-identified. No agreed quantitative threshold for “hegemony succeeded.”

## Scenario Map

No IMF, Fed, ECB, BIS, Eichengreen, Prasad, Atlantic Council, Carnegie, Bruegel, or Valdai source consulted assigns a numeric probability that BRICS or the SCO dethrones the dollar or US hegemony by 2030–2035. Rankings below are labeled **synthesis**.

### Bear Case

De-dollarization stalls. The dollar’s shares in reserves, FX, trade finance, and payments stabilize or rise. A BRICS unit stays dead. RMB internationalization plateaus. India refuses an anti-dollar bloc. Gulf oil and savings stay dollar-heavy. This is a plausible continuation of 2025–26 data, not a minority view. The Fed’s 2025 stock-take found the dollar’s composite international-usage index “basically unchanged since 2022.” [Fed FEDS Note, 18 July 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/the-international-role-of-the-u-s-dollar-2025-edition-20250718.html) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Primary**. PIIE’s September 2026 update found the SWIFT RMB share down from a July 2024 peak of 4.7 percent to 2.75 percent. [PIIE, Chorzempa](https://www.piie.com/blogs/realtime-economics/2026/has-chinese-yuans-rise-global-usage-hit-wall) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Failed prior predictions are the out-of-sample test: 1971 “dethronement,” euro-overtakes-dollar by 2015, RMB premier reserve by ~2020–22, and a BRICS currency. [Chinn and Frankel, NBER w13909](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w13909/w13909.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Subramanian, PIIE WP 11-14](https://www.piie.com/sites/default/files/publications/wp/wp11-14.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

### Base Case (consensus trajectory)

Through 2030–2035 the dollar remains first on every major metric. Its reserve share drifts toward the mid-50s. Lost share goes mostly to non-traditional currencies and gold at the margin, not to the RMB. Intra-BRICS and intra-SCO trade uses more local currency on sanctioned or China-linked corridors. Payment experiments thicken as hedges. US hegemony thins at the edges—sanctions leakage, Global South forum-shopping—without a successor hegemon. The only survey number on a 10-year horizon is OMFIF 2024: reserve managers expect the dollar share “to fall to around 55 percent in 10 years” and the RMB to about 5.6 percent. [OMFIF, 22 October 2024](https://www.omfif.org/2024/10/how-gradual-decline-in-dollar-dominance-could-quicken/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. Eichengreen’s 2025 synthesis is “no dollar crisis or collapse, only a gradual erosion.” [Eichengreen, CIGI 321](https://www.cigionline.org/static/documents/Barry_Eichengreen_phCpxAb.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. This is the explicit near- and medium-term position of the Fed, ECB, Atlantic Council, Prasad, and Eichengreen.

### Bull Case

De-dollarization runs ahead of the OMFIF 55-percent path. Large commodity and intra-South corridors clear outside dollar correspondent banking. US sanctions lose reliability against a peer. Full displacement—RMB, euro, or a BRICS unit as the first global currency by 2030–2035—is a stacked-shock variant, not the core bull case in the named literature. The accelerants the literature actually names are: (1) a US fiscal and institutional shock, including a break in Fed independence; (2) Chinese capital-account convertibility, the missing prerequisite; (3) Gulf savings leaving the dollar, not just a few yuan oil cargoes; (4) an Indian strategic flip. No source puts those four pieces together as the expected path for this decade. [Eichengreen, Project Syndicate, 2023](https://www.project-syndicate.org/commentary/will-us-dollar-global-hegemony-last-by-barry-eichengreen-2023-09) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Prasad, *Foreign Affairs* 2024](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/united-states/top-dollar-currency-prasad) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

The bull case for *hegemony erosion* is easier to populate than the bull case for *dethroning the dollar*. Payment fragmentation can cut US sanctions reach even while the dollar remains first. [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/programs/geoeconomics-center/dollar-dominance-monitor/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**. [Carnegie Politika, 2024](https://carnegieendowment.org/russia-eurasia/politika/2024/10/brics-summit-us-challenge) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High) **Established**.

> Conflicts noted: Fed “no notable reallocation after 2022” versus the 1999–2021 stealth-erosion trend. Conventional sterling lag versus Eichengreen–Flandreau’s faster interwar crossing. ING (petrodollar intact) versus Deutsche Bank/Fortune Hormuz-as-petroyuan scenarios.
> Gaps: No official probabilistic 2030–2035 forecast. No high-frequency official oil-invoice currency series. New Delhi Declaration not yet published.

## Key Findings

According to IMF COFER and the ECB’s June 2026 report, the dollar was still about 57 percent of official FX reserves in early 2026, the euro about 20 percent, and the RMB about 2 percent. According to the BIS 2025 Triennial Survey, the dollar was on one side of 89.2 percent of FX trades, up from 88.4 percent in 2022. This is the first finding: **the challenge is real as rhetoric and as corridor plumbing, not as a completed replacement of the dollar’s core roles.** [IMF Data Brief, July 2026](https://data.imf.org/en/news/imf%20data%20brief%20july%201) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [BIS](https://www.bis.org/statistics/rpfx25_fx.htm) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High).

Official BRICS and SCO texts from Johannesburg through Rio and Tianjin encourage local-currency settlement and a “more representative” order. They do not pledge to replace the dollar. India and official Brazil have spent three years denying a common currency. Russia is the revealed challenger because it was cut off. China builds CIPS while keeping capital controls. This suggests the organisations are **consensus forums with a sanctions-insurance project attached**, not a fused anti-dollar command. [Kazan Declaration](https://cdn.brics-russia2024.ru/upload/docs/Kazan_Declaration_FINAL.pdf?1729693488349783) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [Reuters, Jaishankar](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/brics-have-no-interest-weakening-usd-indian-foreign-minister-says-2024-12-07/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High).

The second finding is institutional. BRICS has no secretariat. Its only treaty money machines are the NDB (a small MDB that still issues dollars) and a CRA that is dollar-denominated, IMF-linked, and unused. The SCO has a secretariat and RATS, not an Article 5. The SCO Development Bank and BRICS Clear remain unbuilt. NDB’s 2025 approvals were $3.2 billion versus World Bank Group commitments of $161.9 billion. [NDB Annual Report 2025](https://www.ndb.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NDB_AnnualReport2025.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/annual-report/financial-summary) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High).

The third finding is legal. OFAC’s secondary-sanctions toolkit is currency-agnostic. Joining SPFS is itself a designation risk. INSTEX failed. CIPS can settle RMB among willing pairs, but any bank that still needs New York correspondents remains inside the perimeter. China is an IMF “freely usable” currency issuer with controls on most capital transactions. There is no binding BRICS or SCO payments code. [OFAC FAQ 1152](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1152) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [IMF Country Report 2026/044](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2026/044/article-A002-en.xml) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High).

The fourth finding is the historical bar. The only completed modern reserve-currency handover took two world wars and a generation, and required a deep safe-asset market plus a crisis lender of last resort. The euro peaked at 28 percent of reserves and stalled for lack of a common Treasury. The yen peaked near 7–9 percent. A currency bloc without a single fiscal authority has never become the *leading* reserve currency. On that scorecard, BRICS and the SCO have not crossed any leadership threshold. [Eichengreen, Tawney Lecture](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~eichengr/research/tawney_lecture2apr29-05.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [Ilzetzki, Reinhart and Rogoff](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w26760/w26760.pdf) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High).

The fifth finding is that **“挑戰美元” and “挑戰美國霸權” are not the same bet, and their success rates diverge.** The consensus trajectory through 2030–2035 is a still-first dollar with a reserve share near the mid-50s (OMFIF’s reserve-manager survey: about 55 percent in ten years). That is a low probability of *dethronement*. The same literature is more open on *hegemony erosion*: Russia already finances a war outside G7 rails; CIPS and bilateral local-currency corridors leak sanctions; BRICS/SCO give Global South states a forum. That is a medium, already-started success at the margin, not a successor order. [OMFIF, October 2024](https://www.omfif.org/2024/10/how-gradual-decline-in-dollar-dominance-could-quicken/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/programs/geoeconomics-center/dollar-dominance-monitor/) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High). [Carnegie Politika, 2024](https://carnegieendowment.org/russia-eurasia/politika/2024/10/brics-summit-us-challenge) (accessed 2026-09-12, confidence: High).

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Treat “金磚／上合取代美元” as a low-probability claim on a 2030–2035 horizon.** Rationale: official reserve, FX, trade-finance, and invoicing series still show dollar primacy; the only 10-year survey number is ~55 percent, not a loss of first place. Evidence: [IMF COFER](https://data.imf.org/en/news/imf%20data%20brief%20july%201); [BIS 2025](https://www.bis.org/statistics/rpfx25_fx.htm); [OMFIF 2024](https://www.omfif.org/2024/10/how-gradual-decline-in-dollar-dominance-could-quicken/).

2. **Watch sanctions leakage and corridor plumbing, not summit slogans.** Rationale: the material change since 2022 is Russia–China (and some Russia–India) local-currency settlement plus CIPS growth, not a BRICS currency. Evidence: [TASS / Overchuk](https://1prime.ru/20250416/overchuk-856733117.html); [CIPS participant announcements](https://www.cips.com.cn/kjjqgsyyingw/2026-07/14/article_2026071413263895335.shtml); [Kazan Declaration](https://cdn.brics-russia2024.ru/upload/docs/Kazan_Declaration_FINAL.pdf?1729693488349783).

3. **Score US self-harm before scoring BRICS cohesion.** Rationale: Eichengreen and Prasad both say the binding upside risk for a faster dollar decline is US fiscal and institutional credibility (Fed independence, debt politics), not a New Delhi communiqué. Evidence: [Eichengreen, Project Syndicate 2023](https://www.project-syndicate.org/commentary/will-us-dollar-global-hegemony-last-by-barry-eichengreen-2023-09); [Prasad, *Foreign Affairs* 2024](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/united-states/top-dollar-currency-prasad); [Fortune on Cook / Fed](https://fortune.com/2026/06/29/supreme-court-fed-independence-lisa-cook-us-debt-treasury-bonds/).

4. **Do not collapse SCO security talk into a NATO or dollar substitute.** Rationale: the Charter is non-alliance; India and Pakistan sit inside; the development bank is unbuilt. Evidence: [SCO Charter](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/LegalTreatiesDoc/000M3130.pdf); [SCO Bishkek Declaration](https://rus.sectsco.org/20260901/2495418.html).

5. **If a single switch matters, it is Chinese convertibility plus an Indian consensus flip.** Rationale: without an open RMB capital account there is no liquid challenger reserve asset; without India there is no BRICS mandate. Both are off the 2026 official path. Evidence: [SAFE / Zhu, June 2026](http://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2026/0617/2439.html); [Goyal / PTI](https://www.fortuneindia.com/economy/india-not-in-favour-of-separate-brics-currency-goyal/152794). This recommendation rests on High-confidence institutional facts and Medium-confidence inference about the *joint* necessity of both switches.
