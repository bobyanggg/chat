# Echigo-Yuzawa / GALA Yuzawa Activities & Experiences

Feb 2027, 3 nights in town. Tailored to **onsen, sake, shopping, evening, and day-trip notes** around a beginner ski trip. Categories adapted for a snow-country station town (not Tokyo nightlife).

Maps pattern (no geocoding): `https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=NAME+Yuzawa+Niigata`

Leave **Status** empty for trip tracking.

## Suggested 3-night activity stack

| When | Do this | Skip this |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Arrival | CoCoLo shop + Ishin or Honjin + **Koji Latte**; optional tasting if you are not skiing at 8:30 next day | Long taxi dinners |
| GALA lesson day | Ski + **GALA base onsen** or **Ponshukan spa** + Mizuya | Uonuma no Sato |
| Ishiuchi day | Full mountain day + reserved izakaya (Kamakura / Yasuyoshi) | Naeba bus |
| Rest / weather hold | Ropeway or Nakazato play + Ponshukan cellar shopping | Forcing GALA in a whiteout/wind hold |
| Departure | Bakudan onigiri + last sake coins; 9:30 shop open | Tight 8:00 Tokyo meeting |
