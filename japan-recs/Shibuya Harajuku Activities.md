# Shibuya / Harajuku Activities & Experiences

Shopping-first day(s) for a **Feb 2027 ski trip**. One easy sight (Meiji Jingu), then fashion, character shops, and tax-free department stores on a walkable Shibuya ↔ Harajuku spine. Indoor malls matter in February.

**Feb 2027 tax-free:** Japan moves to a **refund-at-departure** system from **1 Nov 2026**. Expect to pay tax-in, then refund at the airport after customs checks your goods. Carry your **passport**, keep items/receipts together, and confirm the current ¥5,000-per-store rule before you travel. Same-day same-store minimum still applies.

**Sources:** Time Out Tokyo, TripAdvisor-style itineraries, r/JapanTravel, Japan Real Guide, official store pages.

### Compact 1-day shopping loop (Feb)

1. **Meiji Jingu** at opening → **Takeshita** when shutters go up → **Laforet** / **Kiddy Land** → lunch (**AFURI** or **Beetle**).
2. **Omotesando Hills** + **Cat Street** walk south.
3. **Parco 6F** character floor (weekday) → **109** or **HANDS** → dinner (**Jomon** or **Maisen**).

Weekend backup: skip Takeshita midday; do Parco after 19:00 if Nintendo tickets are gone.
