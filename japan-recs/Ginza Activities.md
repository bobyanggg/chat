# Ginza Activities & Experiences

One easy sight plus department stores, tax-free flagships, stationery, and fashion for a **Feb 2027** ski-trip shopping day. Almost everything is indoor and heated.

**Feb 2027 tax-free:** From **1 Nov 2026** Japan uses a **refund-at-airport** method. Pay tax-in at the register, keep goods + receipts, show them at departure customs, then collect the refund. Passport still required. Typical threshold remains about **¥5,000 tax-excluded per store per day** — confirm before travel. Ginza was ~43% of Tokyo’s Global Blue tax-free sales in 2024; staff here are used to visitors.

**Weekend extra:** Chuo-dori is often a **hokoten** pedestrian paradise on Saturday/Sunday afternoons (roughly 12:00–17:00). Best window-shopping weather even in February.

**Sources:** TripAdvisor (Uniqlo Ginza 2026 reviews), Time Out / GO TOKYO tax-free pages, Global Blue, ByFood, official store sites.

### Compact 1-day Ginza loop (Feb)

1. **Kabukiza** facade or a single act (Higashi-Ginza) → walk west to Chuo-dori.
2. **Mitsukoshi** (depachika + one fashion floor) → **Itoya** → **Kimuraya** snack.
3. **GINZA SIX** + **Dover Street Market** → rooftop pause.
4. **Uniqlo / MUJI / Marronnier Gate** for practical tax-free.
5. Dinner: **Kagari** (queue) or booked **Hirano / Kondo / Ooishi**.

If you only have a half day after skiing gear errands: Uniqlo + Itoya + Mitsukoshi B2 + one dessert (HIGASHIYA or Kimuraya).
