# Ginza Restaurant Recommendations

Luxury-shopping day(s) for a **Feb 2027 ski trip**. Mix of weekday-lunch steals, ramen / tonkatsu between department stores, and one or two reservation-required counters. Desserts are built in for a Chuo-dori break.

**Winter / booking note:** Tempura Kondo, Sushi Suzuki, Kyubey, Bird Land, and Ginza Ooishi need **advance reservations** (often 2–8 weeks; some via OMAKASE / TableCheck). Kagari is walk-in only — go at opening or late afternoon.

**Sources:** r/JapanTravel trip reports, Tabelog Ginza ranking (updated 2025), Time Out Tokyo, ByFood Ginza 2026 guide, TripAdvisor.

### If a place is full

- **Budget tempura / lunch:** keep Abe; otherwise a Mitsukoshi B2 depachika bento (ByFood calls this hall a must).
- **Sushi:** Sushi Harutaka (r/JapanTravel regular) or Hakkoku (Sato; parent of Maguro To Shari).
- **Tempura splurge:** Tempura Fukamachi (r/JapanTravel: vegetables + “Shiso wrapped Uni”; lunch ~¥16,000 is the value) — a short walk toward Kyobashi.
- **Dessert:** Toraya on Chuo-dori, or Pierre Hermé / Henri Charpentier inside GINZA SIX.
