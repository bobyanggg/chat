# Shibuya / Harajuku Restaurant Recommendations

Short Tokyo shopping stop around a **Feb 2027 ski trip** (1–2 days). These 12 spots mix budget / mid / splurge and sit on the Shibuya ↔ Cat Street ↔ Harajuku / Omotesando walk. Always confirm hours and prices before you go.

**Winter note:** indoor counters and depachika seats beat outdoor queues in February. Go early or late for AFURI, Maguro To Shari, and Takeshita dessert lines.

**Sources:** r/JapanTravel, r/Tokyo, Time Out Tokyo (2026), Tabelog, TripAdvisor / itinerary reports.

### If a place is full

- **Budget sushi:** Uogashi Nihon-ichi Dogenzaka (standing sushi; Time Out pick).
- **Tonkatsu / wagyu cutlet:** Gyukatsu Motomura Shibuya (hot-stone finish; Japan Real Guide ~¥2,000).
- **Ramen:** Menya Nukaji (Udagawacho gyokai-tonkotsu from ¥800; Time Out) or Ichiran Spain-zaka.
- **Dessert walk:** Totti Candy Factory (giant cotton candy) or Path in Tomigaya (Dutch pancake; Time Out “sensation”).
