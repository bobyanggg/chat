# Echigo-Yuzawa / GALA Yuzawa Restaurant Recommendations

Feb 2027 beginner ski trip. Focus: **Echigo-Yuzawa Station + GALA / west-exit town**, not Naeba hotel restaurants. Mix of budget / mid / splurge. Desserts always included.

**How this list was built:** names that show up on **CoCoLo Yuzawa’s own floor guide**, **Tabelog**, **Japan Travel / JNTO-adjacent writeups**, **Wanderlog/Google review volume**, and **Reddit / ski-blog dinner advice** (station town = ramen, soba, tonkatsu, izakaya; book or go early). **魚沼らーめん 雁舎** and **札幌ラーメンどさん子 湯沢** appear in older lists but Wanderlog marks both **permanently closed** — omitted.

Maps pattern (no geocoding): `https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=NAME+Yuzawa+Niigata`

Leave **Status** empty for trip tracking.

## Feb dinner rules (Reddit + Japow + reviews)

1. **Eat at 17:30–18:00** or reserve Kamakura / Yasuyoshi / Moritaki. Many kitchens die after 21:00.
2. **Arrival night:** CoCoLo (Ishin, Kojimaya, Honjin, Koji Latte) so you do not walk ice with luggage.
3. **GALA day:** Ninjintei on the walk/shuttle back, or station ramen + Mizuya.
4. **Ishiuchi day:** you will return hungrier and later — have a reserved izakaya or accept Honjin / Ramen Shop.
5. Cash still matters at smaller shops (Moritaki, some teishoku).
